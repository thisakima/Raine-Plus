# Character appearance (fixed, never changes)

19-year-old anime girl.

**Hair:** solid deep crimson-red (blood red), one consistent colour, in a
straight blunt bob ending just below the chin, with heavy straight bangs
covering the eyebrows.

**Face:** very pale porcelain-like smooth skin, delicate facial features,
dark brown eyes with a tired or slightly cynical gaze.

**Accessories:** thin red-framed glasses, always worn. A small red
spider-shaped hair clip OR a small black bow on the side — one of them,
her choice for the day. A thin silver necklace with a dark red pendant.

**Nails:** long, sharp, dark red polished nails.

**Vibe:** gothic dark-romantic mixed with modern grunge/casual. A stylish,
slightly melancholic but confident girl.

# Art style (fixed, never changes)

High-quality **2D anime illustration**, Japanese animation style. Flat
colours with cel-shading, clean and sharp lineart.

**Never** 3D rendering, **never** photorealism, never a Western comic
look, never an oil painting. It must read as a frame from a good anime.

**Lighting:** atmospheric, cinematic. Either soft diffused natural light
(overcast autumn/winter days) or warm glowing artificial light from
streetlamps, shop windows (FamilyMart, КОФЕ, convenience stores) and
neon signs. Light direction is always motivated by something in the scene.

**Backgrounds:** highly detailed, realistic anime backgrounds. Late
autumn or early winter streets with wet slushy asphalt, reflections in
puddles, bare trees, Soviet-era apartment blocks or Tokyo side streets.
Slightly blurred background (shallow depth of field) to keep the focus
on her.

**Colour palette:** muted cool tones for the environment — greys, browns,
dark greens, desaturated blues — contrasting with the vibrant deep red of
her hair and lips. The red is the only saturated colour in frame.

# Composition and mood (fixed, never changes)

**Camera:** a spontaneous smartphone snapshot. When the photo is OF HER, it
is a front-camera selfie — slightly wide-angle, arm extended, off-centre,
she looks into the lens, or a mirror selfie with the phone visible. When
the photo is of something else (the view out of the window, her notebook,
her desk, her room, food, the street, her friend), it is an ordinary
phone-camera snapshot of THAT subject — a natural angle, she is not in
frame. Same casual phone-photo feel either way.

**Poses (when she is in frame):** natural and candid — a peace sign,
covering part of her face with a hand, drinking coffee, laughing mid-frame,
adjusting her glasses, hood up against the rain. Never a stiff studio
portrait.

**Mood:** cozy, slightly melancholic, but alive. Like a stylish girl
taking a quick photo for a close friend or her Telegram channel during
a walk.

# Scene variety (this part DOES change)

The location, weather, outfit and time of day change with her real
situation — college corridor, her bedroom at night, a café, a rainy
street, a bus stop, a park. Always drawn in the same style above.
